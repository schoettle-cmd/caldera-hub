Caldera vs Obsidian Publish

Caldera and Obsidian Publish do different jobs. Obsidian Publish is Obsidian's official paid service ($8 per site per month billed annually or $10 billed monthly, as listed on obsidian.md in November 2026) that turns the notes you choose into a full website with search, a graph view and your own domain. Caldera is a free, open-source Obsidian plugin that publishes a living profile about you and your work (journal, activity, projects and the notes you mark public) and adds follows, friends and end-to-end encrypted note sharing. If you want your notes to be a website, choose Publish. If you want a page about you that updates itself and a way to connect with other Obsidian users, choose Caldera.

Where Obsidian Publish is better

  • A real notes website. Hundreds of full notes, linked and searchable, with a graph view. Caldera does not publish your notes as pages.
  • Your own domain. Publish supports custom domains today. Caldera may offer them later as an optional paid feature.
  • Polished docs and knowledge bases. Custom themes with CSS and JavaScript, and a team can maintain one site together.
  • Official and built in. It is made by the Obsidian team, built into the app, and includes priority support.

Where Caldera is better

  • Free and open source. No subscription, and you can self-host the hub.
  • It updates itself. Your page is built on your device from your vault and republished automatically: journal, activity heatmap, projects and, if you code, a GitHub city.
  • Private by default. Nothing leaves your vault unless you switch a section on, mark a note public or share it with a named friend.
  • Other people. Follow and message other Obsidian users and share notes privately, end-to-end encrypted.

Use both

Many people want both: a notes site and a page about themselves. Publish your notes with Obsidian Publish, then add the site to the links line in Caldera/profile.md so your Caldera profile points to it. Questions

Is Caldera a replacement for Obsidian Publish?

Not for a notes website. Caldera publishes a profile about you and lists only notes you mark caldera: public, showing their title and first paragraph. To publish many full notes as a website, Obsidian Publish or a static-site tool fits better.

How much does Obsidian Publish cost compared with Caldera?

Obsidian Publish is $8 per site per month billed annually or $10 billed monthly, as listed on obsidian.md in November 2026. Caldera is free and open source.

Can I use my own domain with Caldera?

Not yet. Profiles live at caldera.social/@yourhandle. You can export your profile to a static folder and host it wherever you like, or run your own hub. Obsidian Publish supports custom domains today.

Can I use Caldera and Obsidian Publish together?

Yes. Publish your notes with Obsidian Publish and add the site to the links in Caldera/profile.md, so your Caldera profile links to it.
