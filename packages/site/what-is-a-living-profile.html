What is a living profile?

A living profile is a public page about you that updates itself from the work you already do, instead of a bio you rewrite by hand. On Caldera, the free, open-source social network for GitHub and Obsidian, it is built on your own device from your Obsidian vault and republished on its own every few hours and about a minute after you edit Caldera/profile.md, while your GitHub city rebuilds every 6 hours.

What does a Caldera living profile show?

  • Daily journal. A flippable journal fed by caldera_headline, caldera_focus, caldera_wins and caldera_themes in your daily notes. An AI routine can write one entry per workday.
  • Activity. A 26-week heatmap, a daily score, streaks and a growth chart, counted by the plugin from your notes.
  • Now, projects and timeline. Written in Caldera/profile.md, with live links.
  • Topics. Only tags you allow-list are shown, so private names never appear.
  • AI usage. Tokens by model and provider from local Claude Code and Codex logs, counts only.
  • Code. Your GitHub repos as a living GitHub city.
  • Notes you choose. Any note marked caldera: public, with its title and first paragraph.
  • Your own sections. One Markdown note per section in Caldera/modules/ (see custom profile sections).

How does it stay up to date?

The Caldera plugin builds the page on your device from your vault and republishes it automatically. Save a change to Caldera/profile.md and the page updates about a minute later. Daily notes feed the journal, your notes feed the activity heatmap, and your GitHub feeds the city. If you use an AI agent, one setup prompt has it write one journal entry per workday and keep your activity counts current, and it asks you before anything goes public.

What stays private?

The plugin reads your vault locally and never uploads it. Only counts, the sections you switch on, notes you mark caldera: public and journal fields you write are published. Your identity is a key in your vault, not a password account. A private view, which only you can see, adds visits, goals and patterns, pushed encrypted from Obsidian. You can also stay out of the People directory, so your page works only for people with the link.

How do I make one?

  1. Sign up with GitHub for a page and a city with nothing to install, or go straight to Obsidian.
  2. Install Caldera from Obsidian's community plugins, or paste the setup prompt from caldera.social/#install into Claude Code, Codex or Cursor.
  3. Type your name and a handle in the Welcome window. Your page goes live at caldera.social/@yourhandle.
  4. Edit Caldera/profile.md for your headline, photo, links, projects and which sections show.

Questions

What is a living profile?

A public page about you that updates itself from the work you already do, such as your notes, journal and code, instead of a bio you rewrite by hand. Caldera builds one from your Obsidian vault and your GitHub.

Does a living profile publish my notes?

No. Caldera publishes counts, the sections you switch on, journal fields you write and only notes you mark caldera: public, showing their title and first paragraph. The plugin reads your vault on your device and never uploads it.

Do I need Obsidian?

No. Sign up with GitHub and you get a page and a GitHub city with nothing to install. Obsidian adds the parts built from your vault: a daily journal, activity, AI usage and the notes you choose.

Can I use my own domain?

Not yet. Profiles live at caldera.social/@yourhandle. You can export your profile as a static site and host it anywhere, or run your own Caldera hub.

Is it free?

Yes. Your page, your city, the plugin and friends are free and open source.
