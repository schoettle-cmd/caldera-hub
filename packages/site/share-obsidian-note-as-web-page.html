How do I share one Obsidian note as a web page?

To share one Obsidian note as its own web page, use a publishing tool: Obsidian Publish (paid, $8 per site per month billed annually or $10 billed monthly, as listed on obsidian.md in November 2026) or a free static-site option such as Quartz. Caldera, a free, open-source Obsidian plugin, does not turn a full note into a page. It lists the note's title and first paragraph on your public profile, or sends the whole note privately to one friend.

If the full text must be readable in a browser by anyone with the link, Caldera is not the tool for that note. Pick Publish for no build step, or Quartz if you are happy to run a build and host the site yourself.

How do I put a note on my Caldera page?

  1. Install Caldera from Obsidian's community plugins, or paste the setup prompt from caldera.social/#install into Claude Code, Codex or Cursor.
  2. Open the note and add caldera: public to its properties (frontmatter).
  3. Run Publish my profile from the command palette, or wait for the next automatic publish (every 6 hours by default). Your page at caldera.social/@yourhandle then lists the note with its title and first paragraph.
  4. To take it down, remove the property and publish again.

Write the first paragraph so it stands on its own: it is the part people read. For the rest of what your page can show, see how to make a public profile from your vault.

Can I send the whole note to one person instead?

Yes, if that person also uses Obsidian. Follow each other on Caldera and you are friends. Open the note and run Share this note with a friend from the command palette. The note is encrypted on your device with your friend's key, the server stores only ciphertext, and it arrives in their vault under Caldera/Inbox/ as a normal Markdown note. It is a copy, like an email, not a live shared document. Step by step: how to share Obsidian notes privately.

What stays private?

Caldera reads your vault on your device and never uploads it. Only counts, the profile sections you switch on, notes you mark caldera: public and journal fields you write are published. Nothing else leaves your vault unless you switch a section on or share a note with a named friend.

When is a paid publishing tool worth it?

Pay for Obsidian Publish when you want many full notes online as a website with search, a graph view and your own domain, and no build step. If you want a page about you that lists a few notes and updates itself, Caldera does that for free. You can also use both: publish the notes, then add the site to the links line in Caldera/profile.md. Full comparison: Caldera vs Obsidian Publish and free alternatives to Obsidian Publish.

Questions

Can Caldera turn one Obsidian note into its own web page?

No. Caldera shows the title and first paragraph of a note you mark caldera: public on your profile at caldera.social/@yourhandle. To put the full note online as its own page, use a publishing tool such as Obsidian Publish or a free static-site generator like Quartz.

Is there a free way to put one Obsidian note online?

Yes. Quartz is a free, MIT-licensed static-site generator, and digital-garden style community plugins publish selected notes to hosts like Netlify or Vercel. Both need a build or a host account. Obsidian Publish is the paid, built-in option.

How do I send one full note to one person without a public link?

With Caldera, follow each other so you are friends, open the note and run Share this note with a friend. The note is encrypted on your device and arrives in your friend's vault under Caldera/Inbox as a normal Markdown note.

How do I take a note off my Caldera page?

Remove caldera: public from the note's properties, then run Publish my profile, or wait for the next automatic publish (every 6 hours by default).
